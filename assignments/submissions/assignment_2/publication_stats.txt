A one-way analysis of variance examined differences in citation counts across five journals (N = 4,980). Mean citation counts differed significantly by journal, F(4, 4975) = 55.1, p < .001, η² = .042. Journal membership accounted for approximately 4.2% of the variance in citation counts.
Tukey-adjusted post hoc comparisons indicated that articles in Academy of Management Journal received significantly more citations than articles in each of the other four journals. Articles in Academy of Management Review received significantly more citations than those in Journal of Applied Psychology, Journal of Organizational Behavior, and Personnel Psychology. Both Journal of Applied Psychology and Journal of Organizational Behavior had significantly higher citation counts than Personnel Psychology. All these comparisons were significant at p < .001. Citation counts did not differ significantly between Journal of Applied Psychology and Journal of Organizational Behavior, mean difference = 1.44, 95% CI [−10.20, 13.10], p = .997.
Across all articles, publication year was negatively correlated with citation count, r(4978) = −.37, 95% CI [−.39, −.34], p < .001.
